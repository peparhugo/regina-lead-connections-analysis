# Regina Lead Program — Health Burden Endpoint Table

Date: 2026-03-16

## Purpose
Compact operator-grade table for the public bundle and future QALY/DALY implementation.

| Endpoint | Evidence currently in repo | Human burden lens | Public-facing use now | QALY/DALY modeling readiness | Main caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IQ decrement / cognitive loss | Lanphear 2005 pooled analysis; Heidari 2022; Needleman 1990 | developmental harm, learning loss, life-course functioning | Yes | Medium | avoid double-counting with education / earnings |
| ADHD-type burden | Goodlad 2013; Dimitrov 2024; Nehri 2016 | behavioural burden, care burden, service use, school impact | Yes | Medium | overlap with cognitive-development channel |
| Educational / long-run functioning loss | partial indirect support | life-course functioning and adult attainment | Narrative only | Low-Medium | less directly parameterized in current repo |
| Cardiovascular burden | Navas-Acien 2007 + BP effect anchors | chronic disease burden, reduced quality of life, later morbidity | Yes, cautiously | Medium | intermediate-to-final outcome mapping needed |
| CKD burden | review + Harari 2018 | chronic illness burden, long-term health loss | Yes, cautiously | Medium | incidence and duration mapping still needed |
| Mortality / healthy life expectancy burden | partial seed evidence only | years of life lost / healthy life years lost | Not as headline yet | Low | avoid overclaiming before stronger causal mapping |

## Proposed bundle sentence
Lead exposure burden should be read in at least two ways: as a financial burden on households and systems, and as a human burden affecting child development, chronic disease risk, and healthy years of life.
