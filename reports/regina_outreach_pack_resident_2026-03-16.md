## Do / Don’t
### Do
- start with the merged narrative or bundle
- keep the message plain-language and family-oriented
- point people to the city page for direct service information
- keep the focus on recognition, movement, and the open delivery question

### Don’t
- lead with technical appendices or methods docs
- overstate certainty about household-level impact
- treat unresolved testing questions as settled proof
- make the story feel like a technical report first
