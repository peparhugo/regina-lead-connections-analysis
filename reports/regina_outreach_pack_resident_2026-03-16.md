# Regina Lead Service Connections — Outreach Pack (Resident / Community)

Date: 2026-03-16
Audience: Residents, community members, neighbourhood advocates

## Purpose
This pack is meant to be readable quickly and shared easily.

It is designed to answer:
- what happened,
- why it matters,
- where to read more,
- and what residents can check for themselves.

## Safest lead line
Regina recognized the lead-service-line issue years ago. The public question now is whether the response has been fast and strong enough to count as real protection.

## Best angles for this audience
- what changed and what hasn’t
- why this matters for families and neighbourhoods
- where to look up the city’s own lead-service information
- how to move from concern to informed action

## Do / Don’t
### Do
- start with the handout or public brief
- keep the message plain-language and family-oriented
- point people to the city page for direct service information

### Don’t
- lead with methods docs or burden-model appendices
- overstate certainty about household-level impact
- make the story feel like a technical report first
