Quality Gate

JTB: Justified True Belief

Every claim must pass through a review pipeline before it becomes trusted knowledge. The system attacks its own outputs.

1. Intake

Raw worker outputs → normalized claim records with evidence refs, confidence scores, and provenance.

cc-jtb-intake-evidence

2. Build & Review

Claims batched, cross-referenced, contradictions flagged. Review profiles applied per domain.

cc-jtb-build-claim-batch

3. Adjudicate

Each claim gets a verdict: supported, rejected, provisional, or insufficient evidence.

cc-jtb-review-and-revise

Key Design Principle: The Counterargument Monkey

Before any claim is published, a dedicated worker attacks it:

  • What's the strongest counterargument?
  • Does the evidence actually support the claim?
  • What would an opponent say?
  • Is our confidence justified?

Regina example: The counterargument monkey found that councillor accountability claims drop from 0.90 to 0.72 strength under a "budget prudence" attack — but the harm magnitude claims survive at full strength because they're anchored to city data.
