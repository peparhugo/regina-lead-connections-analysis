The Idea Behind It

World Models
→ Belief Models

In 2018, Ha & Schmidhuber showed agents can learn inside their own dreams — building compressed world models, then training on predictions from those models instead of reality.

World Models (Ha & Schmidhuber 2018)

Agent learns inside its own dream

Vobserve
Mmodel
Cdecide

Compress observations → build a predictive model → train a controller entirely inside the model's generated environment. Add noise to prevent the agent from exploiting imperfections in its own dream.

Dream Lab / JTB (our adaptation)

System learns by attacking its own beliefs

📥observe
🐒believe
⚔️attack
✅know

Workers generate candidate beliefs from data → a counterargument agent stress-tests them inside the belief model → JTB review adjudicates what survives. The "noise" is adversarial scrutiny.

The parallel: World Models add noise to prevent dream exploitation. We add a counterargument monkey to prevent belief exploitation.
