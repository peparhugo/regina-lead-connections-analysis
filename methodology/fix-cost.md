# Cost to Fix: $34.4M

## Derivation

| Component | Per connection | Connections | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| City-side replacement | $12,750 | 1,548 | $19,737,000 |
| Private-side replacement | $9,500 | 1,548 | $14,706,000 |
| **Combined** | **$22,250** | **1,548** | **$34,443,000** |

## Sources
- City-side: $36M program for 3,600 connections = $10,000/connection (CR21-90 report, 2021)
- Private-side: $50-70M for 7,000-8,000 connections = $6,250-$10,000 (CTV 2021)
- Inflation adjustment: Saskatchewan construction CPI 2021→2026 ~25-30%
- City-side inflated: $10,000 × 1.275 = $12,750
- Private-side inflated: $7,500 × 1.267 = $9,500 (midpoint)

## Comparison to burden
- Fix cost: $34.4M
- At-risk burden (snapshot): $38.0M
- Ratio: every $1 spent avoids $1.11 in burden
