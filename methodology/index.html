Research Principles

  • ✓ Transparency: All data sources are publicly accessible. No proprietary or restricted datasets.
  • ✓ Reproducibility: Complete SQL queries provided. Results can be independently verified.
  • ✓ Conservative estimates: Parameters chosen to produce lower-bound estimates where uncertainty exists.
  • ✓ Peer-reviewed basis: Health burden calculations grounded in established literature (Lanphear, Gould, EPA LCRI).
  • ✓ Canadian-specific costs: Healthcare costs use CIHI and CADTH guidelines, not US estimates.
  • ✓ Sensitivity analysis: Results presented with ranges reflecting parameter uncertainty.
  • ✓ Limitations acknowledged: Known data quality issues and assumptions explicitly documented.
