Why 696, Not 1,715

This site previously reported 1,715 implied replacements — calculated as 4,458 (baseline) minus 2,743 (claimed remaining). Both the baseline and remaining counts have been corrected through geo-intersection verification, and the replacement methodology has been revised to use city-documented records rather than simple subtraction.

The authoritative replacement count of 696 comes from the City of Regina's filter program yearly progress report, which documents lead service connection (LSC) replacements performed through the city's replacement program. These are confirmed replacements backed by work orders and material-change records.

Assumptions & Limitations

  1. Program records: The 696 LSC replacements cover only 2020–2023. 2024–2025 data was not available in the city's published reports at time of analysis.
  2. Private-side exclusion: Replacements counted are city-side only. Homeowner-initiated private-side replacements are not tracked in city records.
  3. Material inference: Pb→Cu transitions in GIS layers indicate replacement but do not guarantee a physical excavation occurred. Some material code changes may reflect data corrections rather than physical work.
  4. Confidence tiers: Tier 3 (900 candidates) likely overcounts — some material changes are data corrections. Tier 2 (8 high-confidence) likely undercounts — the strict evidence criteria exclude many actual replacements.
