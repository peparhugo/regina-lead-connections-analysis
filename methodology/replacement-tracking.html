Replacement Tracking Methodology

Key figure: 1,715 implied replacements (38.5% of 4,458 baseline)
Method: ArcGIS snapshot comparison — current layer vs. baseline snapshot layer
Last verified: 2026-04-06

Method

Regina's ArcGIS service contains two material-data layers:

  1. Snapshot layer (CBMH Survey): Historical record of material at baseline (~2019).
  2. Current layer (DomesticWaterNetworkTrace): Most recent material for each connection.

When a connection changes from Pb (lead) in the snapshot to Cu (copper) or another non-lead material in the current layer, we infer a replacement occurred.

The city-documented total (931) is a conservative subset. The implied total (1,715) also captures replacements from 2017–2018 and late 2023–early 2026 not yet in published reports.

Corroboration

The Cathedral Area Community Association obtained the official household address list under a September 2025 information request. Independently, the list confirms approximately 2,700 households with lead connections — consistent with the 2,743 ArcGIS count.

Filter program records show 1,919 filters installed across 2020–2023, with annual totals growing from 409 (2020) to 776 (2023).
