Delay Cost: CR21-90 (2031 vs 2036)

The vote

May 26, 2021. Amendment for Option 3 (2031) defeated 4-7.

Source: Council Minutes, iqm2 ID 2818.

Calculation

Under linear replacement assumption:

  • Option 3: all 1,548 unreplaced connections done by 2031
  • Option 2: same total done by 2036
  • At 2031, ~50% of unreplaced still active under Option 2 = 772 connections
  • Those 772 expose children for 5 additional years (2031-2036)
  • Children exposed: 772 × avg(children_014/dwelling) × cohort turnover = 107
  • Burden: 107 × $79,365 = $4,105,963
  • QALYs: 107 × 0.30 = 28 (rounded)

North Central delay cost

North Central bears $825,000 of the $4.1M delay cost (20%).
