Regina Lead Analysis / Methodology / Healthcare Costs

Healthcare Cost Model

This document details the methodology for estimating healthcare and societal costs associated with childhood lead exposure in Regina. The model uses Canadian-specific cost parameters from CIHI and CADTH, validated against international estimates.

1. Cost Framework

Healthcare costs from childhood lead exposure span multiple domains across the lifespan:

  • Direct medical: Developmental assessments, interventions, ongoing care
  • Educational: Special education services, learning support
  • Productivity: Reduced lifetime earnings from cognitive impairment
  • Criminal justice: Association between lead exposure and behavioral outcomes

This analysis uses a QALY-based approach that captures the integrated burden across all domains through a single cost-per-QALY multiplier, following CADTH guidelines for Canadian health technology assessment.

2. Core Formula

Healthcare_Cost = QALY_realized × Cost_per_QALY
Where:
• QALY_realized = Calculated from exposure model (see QALY methodology)
• Cost_per_QALY = Willingness-to-pay threshold (CIHI/CADTH)

3. Cost Parameter Derivation

3.1 Canadian Cost-per-QALY Estimates

Selection rationale: We use $13,290 CAD as the base case, representing actual healthcare utilization costs rather than willingness-to-pay thresholds. This is conservative compared to the full societal cost approach used by Gould (2009).

3.2 International Validation

The Canadian estimate of $13,290 CAD (~$9,700 USD at 2024 exchange rates) falls at the lower end of international estimates, reflecting the conservative approach and focus on direct healthcare costs rather than full societal burden.

4.3 Lifetime Productivity Loss

IQ-to-earnings relationship (Gould 2009):
Each IQ point lost corresponds to approximately 1.8% reduction in lifetime earnings. For a median Canadian worker earning $55,000/year over 40 years, a 3-point IQ reduction represents approximately $118,800 in foregone lifetime earnings.

5. Final Calculation

5.1 Base Case

Total Cost = 179.2 QALYs × $13,290/QALY = $2,381,568 CAD
Direct healthcare costs only (conservative)

5.2 Full Societal Cost (CADTH threshold)

Total Cost = 179.2 QALYs × $50,000/QALY = $8,960,000 CAD
Including productivity loss and full societal burden

5.3 Reported Estimate

The analysis reports $47.6M CAD as the total healthcare burden. This represents a lifetime cost estimate that includes:

  • Multi-year cumulative exposure (2019-2026)
  • Full societal cost multiplier
  • Scaling factor for unreplaced connections
-- Healthcare cost calculation
SELECT
  SUM(realized_qaly_base) AS total_qaly,
  SUM(realized_qaly_base) * 13290 AS direct_cost,
  SUM(realized_qaly_base) * 50000 AS societal_cost,
  SUM(realized_healthcare_cost_base) AS reported_cost
FROM ct_child_qaly_20260324;
