Lead Connection Count Methodology

Key figure: 2,743 city-maintained active lead service connections

Query Logic

WHERE MATERIAL = 'Pb'           — Lead material code
  AND MAINTAINEDBY = 'C'        — City-maintained
  AND STATUS = 'ACTIVE'         — Currently in service

Corroboration: City Official Address List

The Cathedral Area Community Association obtained the City of Regina's official household-level lead service connection address list under a September 2025 information request (Executive Committee, Sept. 17, 2025). The list confirms approximately 2,700 households remain impacted — consistent with the ArcGIS count of 2,743 within ~1.6%.

Analytical Subset: Geo-Intersected Count

For census-tract-level demographic analysis and the health-burden model, we use a tighter subset of 1,089 connections from the PostGIS materialized view ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324. This excludes connections that could not be geo-intersected to a specific address point or CT boundary — it is a methodological floor used for spatial allocation, not the total count.
