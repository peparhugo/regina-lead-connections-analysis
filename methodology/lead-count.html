Query Logic

WHERE MATERIAL = 'Pb'           — Lead material code
  AND MAINTAINEDBY = 'C'        — City-maintained
  AND STATUS = 'ACTIVE'         — Currently in service

Corroboration: Sept 2025 City Address List

The Cathedral Area Community Association obtained the City of Regina's official lead service connection address list and published it as an interactive Google Map (September 2025), presented to the Executive Committee on September 17, 2025 (PDF).

The map contains 2,000 addresses (limited by Google Maps export cap). Cross-referencing against the PostGIS known_lead_address_reconciliation_triaged table (2,307 reconciled addresses) shows a 99.95% match rate, validating the data pipeline. The reconciled address database breaks down as:

MaterialCountPct
Pb (lead — still active)1,37959.8%
Cu (copper — likely replaced)83636.2%
Unknown / other924.0%

Note that the city's FOI dataset reflects the 2021 baseline — the Cathedral Village page states the map "does not reflect replacements that might have occurred after August 2021." The 2,743 ArcGIS active count represents a real-time query that incorporates post-2021 replacements, while the 1,379 Pb address count is from the reconciliation table at address granularity (some addresses have multiple service lines).

Analytical Subset: Geo-Intersected Count

For census-tract-level demographic analysis and the health-burden model, we use a tighter subset of 1,089 connections from the PostGIS materialized view ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324. This excludes connections that could not be geo-intersected to a specific address point or CT boundary — it is a methodological floor used for spatial allocation, not the total count.
