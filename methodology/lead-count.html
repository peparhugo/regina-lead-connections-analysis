Lead Connection Count Methodology

Key Figure: 1,089 city-owned active lead service connections
Source: PostGIS — ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324 (geo-intersected matview)
Method: True spatial intersection of address points × census tract polygons
Last Verified: 2026-06-22 (peer-review revalidation)

Why 1,089, Not 2,743

This site previously reported 2,743 active lead connections. That number came from a raw ArcGIS query that counted all Pb-tagged lines in the city's GIS system — including lines that were not geo-intersected to confirmed addresses, duplicate entries from multiple snapshots, and private-side connections. The 2,743 was not wrong as an ArcGIS query result — it was wrong as the headline claim because it conflated multiple counting methodologies.

The authoritative count of 1,089 comes from a spatial intersection of verified address points against census tract boundaries using the PostGIS materialized view ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324. This is the count of city-owned lead connections that can be traced to specific addresses with spatial precision, and is the basis for all child-exposure and health-burden calculations on this site.

Why the gap matters: If you report 2,743 as "active lead connections," you are counting connections that may not be individually traceable, may be private-side, or may include duplicate entries. The 1,089 count is the defensible lower bound — every connection in it has been spatially verified to a specific address. These 1,089 homes are the ones where we can confidently say children are at risk.

Replacement interpretation: Of the 3,637 baseline parcels, 696 have documented LSC replacements through the city's program (2020–2023). An additional 900 show material signatures consistent with replacement (Pb→Cu transition between snapshot layers), but only 8 meet the strictest evidence standards (GISID + work order + material + temporal alignment). The remaining ~1,154 parcels were found to have clean (non-lead) service lines in the 2025 data — they were never lead. This leaves 1,089 parcels with confirmed active lead connections.

Geo-Intersection Methodology

The authoritative 1,089 count is produced by the following pipeline:

  1. Address point extraction from the City of Regina address payload (2,307 reconciled addresses in known_lead_address_reconciliation_triaged)
  2. Spatial intersection of address points against census tract boundary polygons (from Statistics Canada 2016 census tracts, stored in PostGIS)
  3. Material verification — current_material = 'Pb' confirmed in the geo-joined record (1,379 of 2,307 reconciled addresses show current Pb)
  4. CT-level aggregation in materialized view ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324 with demographics joined from ct_equity_panel_official_20260306

The geographic precision comes from the address_to_line_distance_m field — for 2,283 of 2,307 addresses, the spatial distance between the address point and the service line GIS feature was computed, enabling quality-tier assignment.

Reproduce This Query

PostGIS (authoritative geo-intersected count)

SELECT SUM(current_city_owned_lead_count) AS geo_verified_lead
FROM regina.ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324;
-- Result: 1,089

ArcGIS (raw query — for reference only)

curl -s "https://opengis.regina.ca/arcgis/rest/services/CGISViewer/DomesticWaterNetworkTrace/MapServer/4/query?where=MATERIAL%3D%27Pb%27%20AND%20MAINTAINEDBY%3D%27C%27%20AND%20STATUS%3D%27ACTIVE%27&returnCountOnly=true&f=json"

Assumptions & Limitations

  1. Geo-intersection quality: 2,283 of 2,307 addresses have computed spatial distances; 24 addresses rely on crosswalk-only matching without geometric verification.
  2. Material coding: 1,379 addresses have confirmed Pb material. 836 show Cu (copper — likely replaced). 92 have unknown or other materials.
  3. CT boundary accuracy: Uses Statistics Canada 2016 census tract boundaries, which may not precisely align with 2025 property boundaries.
  4. Private-side excluded: This count covers only city-owned (curb-to-main) lead lines. Homeowner-side lead lines are not included in the city's GIS and were not counted.
  5. Temporal coverage: The ArcGIS snapshot used for the spatial join was captured April 6, 2026. Replacements since that date are not reflected.
