Child Exposure Model

Key Figure: ~1,540 children (ages 0-14) in affected census tracts
Method: CT-level spatial join of lead connections to StatsCan demographics
Last Calculated: 2026-04-06

Model Overview

We estimate child exposure by:

  1. Spatially joining lead service connections to census tract (CT) boundaries
  2. Applying StatsCan demographic data (children 0-14) at CT level
  3. Weighting by lead connection density within each CT

Formula

Impacted_Children_CT = Children_0_14_CT × (Lead_Connections_CT / Total_Connections_CT) × Exposure_Weight

Where:

  • Children_0_14_CT: StatsCan 2016 count of children aged 0-14 in the census tract
  • Lead_Connections_CT: Count of city-owned ACTIVE Pb lines in the CT (from spatial join)
  • Total_Connections_CT: Total water connections in the CT
  • Exposure_Weight: Conservative factor (0.8) assuming not all children in lead-connected homes are equally exposed

Spatial Join Method

The household-to-CT spatial join is performed in PostGIS:

-- Materialized view: ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324
SELECT
  ct.ctuid,
  ct.ctname,
  count(DISTINCT addr.gisid) as lead_connections,
  sum(ct.children_0_14 * exposure_weight) as impacted_children
FROM census_tracts ct
JOIN address_points_crosswalk addr
  ON ST_Contains(ct.geom, addr.geom)
JOIN water_lines wl
  ON addr.gisid = wl.gisid
WHERE wl.material = 'Pb'
  AND wl.maintainedby = 'C'
  AND wl.status = 'ACTIVE'
GROUP BY ct.ctuid, ct.ctname;

Assumptions & Limitations

  1. Census data age: 2016 Census is the most recent with CT-level child counts; actual 2026 demographics may differ
  2. Household occupancy: Assumes households with lead connections have similar child density to CT average
  3. Exposure uniformity: Does not account for filter usage, water consumption patterns, or building age
  4. Conservative estimate: The 0.8 exposure weight intentionally underestimates to avoid overclaiming

Reproduce This Analysis

# Connect to PostGIS
docker exec -it regina-postgis-20260324 psql -U regina -d regina

# Query the CT synthesis view
SELECT ct_name, current_city_owned_lead_count,
       impacted_children_0_14_est
FROM regina.ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324
ORDER BY impacted_children_0_14_est DESC;
