Policy Implications

  • Renters cannot initiate replacement: Unlike homeowners, renters depend on landlords or city programs
  • No provincial landlord disclosure requirement: Saskatchewan has no lead-specific disclosure rules
  • Income barriers: Low-income households less likely to afford bottled water or point-of-use filters
  • Filter program: 27% of lead-connection addresses had filter coverage in 2024, up from 13% in 2022 (City of Regina LSC program report, Sept 2025 Executive Committee presentation). 1,919 filters installed 2020–2023.

Reproduce This Analysis

# PostGIS query for CT equity panel
docker exec -it regina-postgis-20260324 psql -U regina -d regina -c "
SELECT ct_name,
       current_city_owned_lead_count as lead,
       tenure_renter_pct,
       lim_at_prevalence_pct,
       median_after_tax_income_households
FROM regina.ct_direct_lead_synthesis_20260324
WHERE current_city_owned_lead_count > 0
ORDER BY current_city_owned_lead_count DESC
LIMIT 10;"
