# QALY Derivation

## Claim
At-risk: 480 children × 0.30 QALY/child = 144 QALYs

| Mild anxiety | 0.030 | Above our estimate |
| ADHD | 0.045 | Above our estimate |
| Mild intellectual disability | 0.043 | Above our estimate |

## 30-year NPV
IQ loss is permanent (Reuben et al. 2017, 38-year Dunedin cohort follow-up).
NPV factor at 3% discount rate for 30 years: 20.19
QALY per child: 0.015 × 20.19 = 0.30
= 111 days of full health lost per child over 30 years.
